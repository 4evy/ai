# keys

## keys

A single printable Unicode character or a named key; Enter, Tab, Escape,
Backspace, Space, navigation, F1–F35, KP_/Keypad/Numpad keys, locks,
PrintScreen, Pause, Menu, media/volume and left/right modifier keys. Combine
Shift, Alt/Option, Ctrl/Control, Super/Cmd/Command/Win, Hyper, Meta, CapsLock
and NumLock in any order; names are case-insensitive. Literal + is + or Plus
(Alt++ also works). Extended combinations require the application's Kitty or
xterm modifyOtherKeys mode; unsupported chords are rejected, not approximated
